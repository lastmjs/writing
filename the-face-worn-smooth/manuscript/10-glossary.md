# Glossary
## 用語

**Ave Maria.** The "Hail Mary." Hidden Christians kept it in Latin and Japanese forms, and it was among the first prayers children learned.

**Bastian.** A Japanese catechist of the Sotome coast, remembered in hidden-Christian tradition as the author of their church calendar and of a prophecy that the faith would be restored after seven generations.

**Chōkata** (帳方). "Keeper of the book." The lay leader of a hidden-Christian community, who kept the liturgical calendar and announced its feasts and fasts.

**Credo.** The Apostles' Creed.

**Deus** (デウス). God. Japanese Christians used the Latin word.

**Dochirina Kirishitan** (どちりなきりしたん). "Christian Doctrine." A catechism in question-and-answer form, printed by the Jesuit press in Japan in 1591–92 and 1600.

**E-fumi** (絵踏). "Picture-treading." The ritual of making people step on a sacred image to prove they were not Christian.

**Fumi-e** (踏絵). "Stepping-picture." The image used in e-fumi: first on paper, then a wooden board set with a medal, and from 1669 a cast-brass plaque.

**Kan'ei, Meireki, Manji, Kanbun, Genji, Meiji.** Japanese era names. The story runs from Meireki 3 (1657) to Kanbun 10 (1670), with a prologue memory of Kan'ei 6 (1629) and an epilogue in Genji 2 (1865).

**Kirishitan** (キリシタン). Christian, from the Portuguese *cristão*.

**Konchirisan no ryaku** (こんちりさんのりやく). "A Summary of Contrition," compiled by the Jesuits in 1603 and copied by hand for Christians who would have no priest to hear their confessions.

**Kuzure** (崩れ). "Collapse." The authorities' word for the exposure and mass arrest of a hidden-Christian community.

**Mizukata** (水方). "The one of the water." The lay baptizer of a hidden-Christian community.

**Nishizaka** (西坂). The hill above Nagasaki harbor where the Twenty-Six Martyrs were crucified in 1597 and many hundreds of Christians were executed afterward.

**Orasho** (おらしょ). Prayer, from the Latin *oratio*.

**Otona / ward elder** (乙名). The headman of a ward of Nagasaki, responsible for its registers and for conducting e-fumi.

**Paraiso** (パライソ). Paradise; heaven.

**Pater Noster.** The Lord's Prayer. In the *Dochirina*: *Ten ni mashimasu warera ga on-oya* (天にましますわれらが御親), "Our Father who art in heaven."

**Tomasu** (トマス). Thomas, the apostle who said, "Unless I see... I will not believe," and then, "My Lord and my God."

**Zezus** (ゼズス). Jesus, in the Portuguese-influenced pronunciation of the Kirishitan.
